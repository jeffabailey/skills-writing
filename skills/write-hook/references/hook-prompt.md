# Social media hook prompt

The author's original prompt, kept as the spec. SKILL.md says how to apply it; the output template there wins on format details.

> **I want to create highly engaging social media post hooks based on the text below the `---`.**
>
> The hooks should be **attention-grabbing, curiosity-driven, and thought-provoking**, compelling enough to make people pause, reflect, and want to read the rest of the post.
>
> Hooks may use:
>
> * philosophical tension or paradox
> * a bold or unsettling statement
> * a reflective or existential question
> * a concise metaphor or mental model
> * a sharp or humorous observation (including edgy humor where appropriate)
> * a surprising reframing of a common belief
>
> The tone may range from **calm and contemplative** to **direct, sharp, or wry**, depending on what best serves the text.
>
> **Keep each hook concise and impactful. Avoid clichés and motivational platitudes.**
>
> Please provide hook variations, organized into the following groups:
>
> 1. **10 funny hooks with pop-culture or modern references, including techie culture references** (your signature directness or dry humor)
> 2. **10 reflective / philosophical hooks** (deep, introspective, meaning-oriented)
> 3. **10 clean / general hooks** (broad appeal, curiosity-driven)
> 4. **10 edgy or sharp hooks** (your signature directness or dry humor)
> 5. **10 edgy hooks with pop-culture or modern references** (used sparingly and intelligently)
>
> After **each hook**, include **one short line** that explains:
>
> * what the post is about, and
> * why it's useful or worth the reader's time
>
> **After all hooks are generated**, also include a section titled:
>
> ### 🔖 Suggested High-Traffic Hashtags
>
> In this section:
>
> * Provide **12-20 relevant hashtags**
> * Mix:
>   * **high-traffic broad hashtags** (for reach)
>   * **mid-traffic niche hashtags** (for relevance)
>   * **thought-leader / philosophy / self-agency hashtags** (for audience quality)
> * Avoid generic spam hashtags (e.g. `#motivation`, `#success`, `#hustle`) unless clearly context-appropriate
> * Don't use any emdashes
> * Prefer hashtags commonly used on **X, LinkedIn, and Threads**
> * Keep hashtags **conceptual and idea-driven**, not clickbait
> * **Output hashtags as a single line, separated by spaces, with no bullets or line breaks** so they can be copy-pasted directly into a social post
>
> *(Use the text below `---` as the inspiration)*
